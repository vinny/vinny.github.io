Privacy Policy - NoiseScope

Last updated: August 1, 2025

Your privacy is our priority. This Privacy Policy explains how the NoiseScope app collects, uses, and protects your information. By using our app, you agree to the terms described below.

1. Information Collection

NoiseScope does NOT collect, record, store, transmit, or share any personal user information.

Microphone Usage

  • The microphone is used locally only to measure ambient sound volume (decibel level).
  • No audio is recorded, transmitted, or saved.

Device Data

  • The app does not access any personal data, such as contacts, location, photos, or files.
  • Basic technical information such as device model or Android version may be used anonymously via Google Play Console for statistical and compatibility purposes.

2. Information Sharing

NoiseScope does not share data with third parties.

The image capture and sharing feature is optional and controlled by you. Images are not sent to external servers.

3. Cookies and Similar Technologies

This app does not use cookies or tracking technologies.

4. Advertising and Third Parties

Currently, the available version of the app does not display ads and is free of trackers.

If future versions include advertising, this policy will be updated.

5. Security

We take measures to protect the app's execution, but remember that no technology is 100% secure. We recommend keeping your Android updated.

6. User Rights

Since we don't collect personal data, we don't store information that can be requested, corrected, or deleted.

If this changes in the future, you will be informed in advance.

7. Privacy Policy Changes

We may update this policy at any time. Relevant changes will be communicated in the app or on the Play Store.
