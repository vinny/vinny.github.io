Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. How does story generation work in the app?

Once Animated creates custom stories automatically based on your selections. You choose characters, scenarios, magic objects, and effects, and our system generates a unique 10-page story with captivating text and titles.

2. What free resources are available?

The app offers 25 characters, 15 scenarios, 15 magic objects, and 15 special effects completely free.

3. What does the PRO package add?

The PRO package unlocks 60 exclusive characters, 12 special scenarios, 12 magic objects, 12 special effects, unlimited story creation, and MP3/PDF exports.

4. How does story narration work?

Simply press the play button on the reading screen to listen to the story narrated with a clear and expressive voice.

5. Can stories be read offline?

Yes! Once a story is generated online, it is saved in your personal library and can be read offline anytime.

6. How do I save my favorite stories?

All created stories are automatically saved in your library. Tap the heart icon on any story to mark it as a favorite.

7. Does the app work on tablets?

Yes, Once Animated is fully compatible with Android smartphones and tablets.

8. Where do I find my generated stories?

All your stories are accessible anytime in the Library tab in the app's bottom menu.

9. How can I export and share my stories?

With the PRO package, you can export and share your stories as PDF documents or MP3 audio files.

10. Does the app consume a lot of mobile data?

Story generation requires an internet connection and uses a minimal amount of data. Reading saved stories offline uses zero data.

11. How do I purchase the PRO package?

You can purchase the PRO package directly in the app store section using Google Play billing.

12. Can I adjust the font size?

Yes, app settings allow you to customize font size for comfortable reading.

13. What ages is the app recommended for?

Once Animated is designed for children aged 4 to 10, but fun for the whole family.
