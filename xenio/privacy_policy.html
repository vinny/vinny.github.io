Privacy Policy - XeniO

Last updated: September 29, 2025

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your personal information when you use the XeniO application.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Automatically Collected Information

XeniO automatically collects the following information during app usage:

  • Game statistics (wins, losses, draws)
  • Match history (game type, opponent, result, date and duration)
  • User preferences (selected theme, sound settings)
  • Premium purchase status (if applicable)

1.2 User-Provided Information

During app usage, you may choose to provide the following information:

  • Player names (for identification in matches)

2. How We Use Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • Provide and improve game functionality
  • Maintain and display performance statistics
  • Personalize user experience
  • Process in-app purchases for premium features
  • Store user preferences between sessions

3. Information Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or transfer your personally identifiable information to third parties. The following exceptions apply:

  • Google Play Services: To process in-app purchases, we may share necessary information with Google Play services
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose information if required by law or in response to valid legal processes

4. Data Storage

All collected information is stored locally on your device using Android's SharedPreferences mechanism. No personal information is stored on external servers.

5. In-App Purchases

XeniO offers a one-time purchase to unlock premium features:

  • Benefits: Unlock seasonal themes
  • Payment processed through Google Play Billing
  • We do not collect payment information - this is handled exclusively by Google Play

6. User Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Request deletion of your personal information
  • Revoke consent for data processing (when applicable)

To exercise these rights, you can clear the app data in your Android device settings.

7. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

8. Policy Changes

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify you about any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.

This Privacy Policy applies only to the XeniO application and not to third-party websites or services linked to it.
