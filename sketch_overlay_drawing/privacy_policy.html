Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Sketch Overlay Drawing ("we", "us", or "our") handles your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Privacy First Philosophy

We believe that your creative process is your business alone. Our application is designed with Privacy-by-Design principles. We do not track you, we do not show invasive ads, and most importantly, we do not have access to your creative work.

2. Data Collection and Use

Sketch Overlay Drawing does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to our servers. All information remains strictly on your device.

  • Camera Access: We request camera permission solely to provide the "overlay" functionality. The camera feed is used only to display a live preview of your paper under the drawing template. We do not record or transmit any video or images from your camera.
  • Local Storage: When you save a sketch or use your own images, they are stored locally on your device. We have no access to your gallery or the images you choose to draw.

3. Offline Functionality

Our app respects your connectivity. Active internet connection is NOT mandatory for the core functionality of the app. You can use Sketch Overlay Drawing entirely offline by using your local images or pre-downloaded templates.

4. Third-Party Services (Image Libraries)

To provide an extensive library of drawing references, our app integrates with several image repository APIs, including Pixabay, Unsplash, and Pexels. When you use the search feature:

  • Your search queries are sent to these providers to retrieve relevant images.
  • No personal identification or device information is shared with these services during these requests.
  • Content Safety: All search results are passed through strict filters to ensure they are safe and appropriate for all ages.
  • Usage of images from these providers is subject to their respective terms: Pixabay, Unsplash, and Pexels.

5. No Data Sale or Sharing

We strictly do not sell, rent, or share any information about our users with third parties. Since we do not collect personal data, there is no data to be shared or sold.

6. Security

Because all your data stays on your device, its security is tied to your operating system's security features. We recommend keeping your device updated for the best protection.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through our official support channels.
