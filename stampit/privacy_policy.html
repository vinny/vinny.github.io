Privacy Policy

StampIt! - Your Digital Scrapbook

Last Updated: April 27, 2026

Welcome to StampIt!. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy outlines our practices regarding information handling for our mobile application.

1. Offline Operation & Data Storage

StampIt! is designed to function entirely offline. All your data, including photos, stamp collections, and preferences, are stored locally on your device.

  • No account creation is required to use the app.
  • Your creative content never leaves your smartphone unless you explicitly choose to share it via third-party apps.

2. No Data Collection or Selling

We believe your business is your business. We follow a strict Zero-Data Collection policy:

  • We do not collect, store, or monitor any personal information.
  • We do not track your location or usage behavior.
  • We do not sell, rent, or trade any data to third parties.

3. Permissions

To provide its core features, StampIt! requires certain permissions. These are used strictly in accordance with Google Play and Android security policies:

  • Camera: Required to take photos that will be transformed into digital stamps.
  • Storage/Gallery: Required to save your generated stamps to your device and to import existing photos.
  • Haptic Feedback: Used to provide a premium tactile experience during interactions.

4. Security

Since all data is stored locally, the security of your information depends on the security of your physical device. We recommend using standard device protection (Passcode, Biometrics) to keep your local data safe.

5. Third-Party Services

Our app may contain links to external sites (such as for FAQs or support). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those external entities. We use standard libraries for app functionality which also comply with industry privacy standards.

6. Children's Privacy

StampIt! does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children. It is safe for all ages as a creative tool.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in a new version of the app and updated on this page.
